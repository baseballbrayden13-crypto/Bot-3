# clip-bot Terms of Service

Last updated: November 10, 2026

clip-bot is a personal, non-commercial tool used only by its owner to post
videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and
no one else can sign in to it.

1. **Who can use it.** Only the owner. No other accounts are connected.
2. **Content.** clip-bot only uses content from creators who publicly allow
   clipping of their content, and every video credits the original creator.
   Creators can ask for their content to be removed at any time by opening an
   issue on this repository, and it will be removed.
3. **TikTok rules.** All posts follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community
   Guidelines.
4. **No warranty.** The tool is provided as is.
5. **Changes.** These terms may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
