# clip-bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

clip-bot is a personal tool that posts videos to its owner's own TikTok account.

**What it collects.** Only the TikTok login tokens the owner grants it, used
to upload videos to the owner's account. It does not collect, store or sell
any information about anyone else, and it does not track viewers.

**How tokens are stored.** Tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in GitHub
Actions and are only used to upload videos. They are never shared.

**Removing access.** The owner can disconnect clip-bot at any time in TikTok's
settings (Security and permissions, Apps), which deletes its access.

**Children.** clip-bot is not directed at children and collects no data from them.
